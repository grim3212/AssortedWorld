# Changelog

## 9.1.0

- Added a support for the Instruction Manul
- Brought four pieces of GrimPack's Grim World over:
  - Glowstone seeds. Plant them on a netherrack ceiling and they ripen into a blob of glowstone.
    Craftable from glowstone dust and soul sand.
  - Floating islands. Rather than being a scoop of the ground below it we place build the islands so the ground is left intact.
  - Desert wells. A shaft 10 to 30 blocks deep down to a chest, with loot that climbs with the depth.
    Vanilla's own desert wells are untouched; these generate alongside them.
  - World gen expanded: wild wheat fields, melons, stray saplings, tree stumps, cactus fields,
    sandstone pillars and sand pits.
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.1.0.
